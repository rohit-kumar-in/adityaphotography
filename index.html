OUR CULTURAL HERITAGE

Sacred Indian Wedding Traditions We Preserve

Every Indian wedding is a sacred bond between two families. We document every ritual with cultural reverence, traditional grace, and zero Western clichés.

01 • SAGAI & TILAK

Ring Ceremony & Tilak

The auspicious beginning of the union—capturing the sacred Tilak, shagun thal, elders’ ashirwad, and the joyous Ring Ceremony celebrated with both families.

02 • SACRED CANOPY

Marwa & Matkor

Honoring ancestral customs as the sacred bamboo, banana-pillar, and mango-leaf Marwa canopy is erected amidst traditional folk songs and sacred kalash rituals.

03 • AUSPICIOUS GLOW

Haldi & Ubtan

Vibrant mornings of yellow silk sarees, mogra floral ornaments, brass urlis, and heartfelt blessings as elders apply sacred turmeric and dub grass under the Marwa.

04 • BRIDAL SHRINGAR

Mehendi & Sangeet

Fine-art macro portraits of intricate bridal Mehendi, red and gold chura/lac bangles, and warm musical evenings filled with traditional dholak geet and family dances.

05 • GARLAND EXCHANGE

Mala Badal (Jaimala)

Following the royal Barat welcome and Parchhawan, the bride and groom respectfully exchange lotus and rose garlands under a shower of golden marigold petals.

06 • SACRED VOWS

Siromani, Sindoor & Bidai

The spiritual heart of the wedding: Kanyadaan, Saptapadi Pheras around the holy Agni, the sacred crowning ritual of Siromani & Sindoor Daan, and the emotional Bidai.

06 / THE ATELIER STORY

Behind Every Frame, There’s a Story.

At Aaditya Raj Photography & Videography, we believe an Indian wedding is a sacred spiritual and family milestone—never a staged performance.

Our heart lies in documenting the true soul of Indian traditions: the auspicious erection of the bamboo Marwa, the laughter and blessings of elders during Haldi, the intricate artistry of Mehendi and the Ring Ceremony, the royal celebration of Mala Badal, and the deep reverence of Kanyadaan, Saptapadi Pheras, Siromani, and Bidai.

We honor family dignity and cultural grace in every portrait, ensuring your wedding album and films feel authentically Indian and deeply personal for generations to come.
